# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]  
**Last Updated:** [Last Updated Date]

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules governing how all users — Customers and Service Providers — may use the ServiceHub platform. By using the Platform, you agree to comply with this AUP in full. Violations may result in account suspension, termination, and/or legal action.

---

## 1. Purpose

ServiceHub is a marketplace platform designed to connect Customers with qualified local Service Providers for legitimate home and personal services. This AUP exists to protect users, maintain the integrity of the Platform, and ensure compliance with applicable laws.

---

## 2. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on ServiceHub:

### 2.1 Illegal Services
You may not use the Platform to:
- Offer, request, or facilitate any service that is illegal under Philippine law or the laws of any applicable jurisdiction;
- Advertise, promote, or arrange for the provision of controlled substances, stolen goods, unlicensed firearms, or any regulated items;
- Engage in human trafficking, sexual exploitation, or any form of forced labor;
- Offer services for activities that violate health, safety, environmental, or building regulations.

### 2.2 Fraud and Deception
You may not:
- Create fake, duplicate, or impersonation accounts;
- Misrepresent your identity, qualifications, credentials, or business details;
- Submit false, fabricated, or manipulated documents during verification;
- Engage in payment fraud, including unauthorized use of another person's payment method;
- Abuse the refund or dispute system by making false claims;
- Engage in price gouging or bait-and-switch pricing tactics.

### 2.3 Spam and Unsolicited Communications
You may not:
- Send unsolicited marketing messages, promotions, or solicitations to other users;
- Use the in-app messaging system for purposes unrelated to a legitimate service booking;
- Flood the Platform with repetitive, irrelevant, or automated messages;
- Use contact information obtained through the Platform to communicate outside of the Platform for commercial purposes.

### 2.4 Fake or Manipulated Accounts
You may not:
- Register multiple accounts to circumvent a ban, suspension, or platform limitations;
- Use bots, scripts, or automated tools to register, log in, or interact with the Platform;
- Sell, transfer, or share your account credentials with another person;
- Create accounts on behalf of another person without their explicit consent.

### 2.5 Platform Abuse
You may not:
- Attempt to reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble any part of the Platform;
- Scrape, crawl, or extract data from the Platform using automated tools;
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to any account, system, or network associated with ServiceHub;
- Introduce malware, viruses, ransomware, or other harmful code into the Platform;
- Conduct denial-of-service (DoS) attacks or any action that interferes with Platform availability;
- Exploit security vulnerabilities without prior written authorization from ServiceHub;
- Circumvent geographic restrictions or other technical access controls.

### 2.6 Data Misuse
You may not:
- Collect, store, or process personal information of other users beyond what is strictly necessary for a booked service;
- Use personal information of other users for purposes other than completing the booked service;
- Sell, rent, or disclose other users' personal information to third parties;
- Use user data obtained from the Platform to build competing products or services;
- Violate any applicable data privacy law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

### 2.7 Intellectual Property Violations
You may not:
- Upload, post, or transmit content that infringes any copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret, or other intellectual property right;
- Use ServiceHub's name, logo, branding, or trademarks without prior written permission;
- Reproduce or distribute any content from the Platform without authorization.

### 2.8 Harmful Content
You may not post, share, or transmit content that:
- Is obscene, pornographic, or sexually explicit;
- Promotes violence, terrorism, or self-harm;
- Contains hate speech targeting individuals or groups based on protected characteristics;
- Is designed to defame, humiliate, or harass a specific individual.

## 4. Enforcement

ServiceHub reserves the right to take any of the following actions for violations of this AUP:

- **Content Removal**: Remove or restrict any content that violates this AUP;
- **Warning**: Issue a formal notice to the user;
- **Temporary Suspension**: Restrict Platform access for a defined period;
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanently terminate the account;
- **Legal Action**: Refer the matter to law enforcement or pursue civil remedies;
- **Cooperation with Authorities**: Disclose relevant information to law enforcement agencies when required by law or where public safety is at risk.

The appropriate enforcement action will be determined at ServiceHub's sole discretion based on the nature and severity of the violation.

## 5. No Unlawful Circumvention

Any attempt to circumvent the terms of this AUP — including by creating new accounts, using a third party's account, or technical workarounds — is itself a violation of this policy and will be treated accordingly.
