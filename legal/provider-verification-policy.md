# Provider Verification Policy

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]  
**Last Updated:** [Last Updated Date]

ServiceHub is committed to building a safe and trustworthy marketplace. This Provider Verification Policy ("Policy") outlines the requirements, process, and standards we apply to verify all Service Providers before they may accept bookings on the Platform.

---

## 1. Why We Verify Providers

Provider verification serves to:
- Confirm the true identity of individuals offering services;
- Ensure Providers are legally authorized to perform their offered services;
- Protect Customers from fraud, misrepresentation, and unqualified service workers;
- Maintain the quality and reputation of the ServiceHub marketplace;
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

All Service Providers **must successfully complete verification** before their profile is made visible to Customers or before they may accept any bookings.

---

## 2. Required Documents

All Service Providers must submit the following documents to be verified:

### 2.1 Government-Issued Photo ID
- One (1) valid, government-issued photo ID showing your full legal name, date of birth, and photograph.
- **Accepted IDs include:**
  - Philippine Passport
  - Driver's License (LTO)
  - SSS ID or UMID
  - PhilHealth ID
  - Voter's ID / National ID (PhilSys)
  - PRC License
  - Postal ID

**Requirements:**
- ID must be valid and not expired.
- Both front and back of the ID must be clearly photographed.
- The full ID must be visible within the frame, with no cropping.
- Images must be clear, well-lit, and legible.

### 2.2 Selfie with ID
- A clear, recent photograph of you holding the government-issued ID next to your face.
- Both your face and the ID must be fully visible and clearly legible.
- No filters, heavy editing, or obscured features are acceptable.

---

## 3. Optional Supporting Documents

While not required for basic verification, the following documents can enhance your Provider profile, increase Customer trust, and improve your visibility on the Platform:

### 3.1 Business Permit / Mayor's Permit
- A valid Business Permit issued by your local government unit (LGU).
- Must show your registered business name, address, and validity period.
- Strongly recommended for Providers operating as a registered business.

### 3.2 Professional Certifications or Trade Licenses
Examples include, but are not limited to:
- PRC License (for licensed professions such as engineers, technicians)
- TESDA National Certificate (NC) for skilled trades (plumbing, electrical, welding, etc.)
- BIR Certificate of Registration
- Any relevant industry-specific certification or license

Providers with verified certifications may be awarded a **"Certified Professional" badge** on their profile, increasing Customer confidence.

---

## 4. Document Submission Process

1. **Create a Provider Account**: Register on ServiceHub and select the "Service Provider" role.
2. **Access the Provider Application**: Navigate to your account settings and select "Provider Application."
3. **Upload Required Documents**: Upload clear images of all required documents.
4. **Submit for Review**: Submit your application. You will receive an in-app notification confirming receipt.
5. **Await Approval**: Our verification team will review your submission (see Section 5 for timelines).
6. **Receive Decision**: You will be notified of the outcome via in-app notification and email.

---

## 5. Background Review Process

Upon receiving your application, our verification team will:

- **Verify ID Authenticity**: Cross-check the information on submitted IDs for consistency and authenticity.
- **Facial Verification**: Compare the selfie-with-ID photograph to the ID photo to confirm identity.
- **Document Integrity**: Check for signs of tampering, editing, or forgery.
- **Credential Review**: For optional documents, verify that certifications are valid and current.

**Review Timeline:**
- Standard applications are reviewed within **1 to 3 business days**.
- Applications requiring additional verification may take up to **5 business days**.

---

## 6. Approval Process

If your application meets all requirements:
- Your account status will be updated to **"Verified Provider."**
- Your profile will become visible to Customers in your service area.
- You will be eligible to receive and accept booking requests.
- A verification badge may be displayed on your public profile.

You will receive an in-app notification and email confirming your approval.

---

## 7. Rejection Process

Your application may be rejected if:
- Submitted documents are unclear, blurry, or partially obscured;
- The ID is expired or invalid;
- The selfie does not match the ID photo;
- There are signs of document manipulation or forgery;
- The submitted information does not match across documents;
- You do not meet the minimum eligibility requirements.

**If Rejected:**
- You will receive a notification explaining the reason for rejection.
- You may resubmit your application with corrected or new documents.
- Deliberate submission of fraudulent documents will result in **permanent account termination** and may be reported to authorities.

---

## 8. Re-Verification Requirements

ServiceHub reserves the right to require re-verification in the following circumstances:
- Your documents expire and we request updated copies;
- We have reason to believe your submitted information is no longer accurate;
- Changes to platform policy require additional verification;
- Unusual activity or complaints have been filed against your account.

Failure to complete re-verification within the specified timeframe may result in account suspension until verification is renewed.

---

## 9. Ongoing Compliance Requirements

Verification is not a one-time event. Verified Providers are expected to:
- Maintain all required licenses, permits, and certifications for their offered services;
- Update their documents on the Platform promptly when any document expires or changes;
- Notify ServiceHub of any criminal conviction, professional license suspension, or other material change in their eligibility to operate;
- Continue to meet the conduct and performance standards outlined in the Provider Terms and Conditions.

---

## 10. Data Privacy

All documents submitted for verification are handled in strict accordance with our **Privacy Policy** and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Verification documents are:
- Stored securely with access restricted to authorized verification personnel;
- Used exclusively for identity and eligibility verification purposes;
- Not shared with Customers or third parties, except as required by law;
- Retained in accordance with our data retention schedule.
