## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give website owners information about how their site is being used.

Cookies may be:
- **Session cookies**: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.
- **Persistent cookies**: Cookies that remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted.
- **First-party cookies**: Set by ServiceHub directly.
- **Third-party cookies**: Set by external services integrated into the Platform (e.g., analytics or payment providers).

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are **strictly necessary** for the Platform to function. Without them, core services such as logging in, maintaining your session, and processing bookings cannot operate.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `session_token` | Maintains your authenticated login session | Session |
| `csrf_token` | Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks | Session |
| `auth_user` | Identifies your logged-in account | 30 days |
| `locale` | Remembers your language/region preference | 1 year |

**You cannot opt out of essential cookies** as they are required for the Platform to operate. Disabling them may prevent you from accessing the Platform.

### 2.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the Platform — which pages are visited most, how users navigate between screens, and where errors occur. This information helps us improve performance and user experience.

| Cookie Name / Tool | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics (`_ga`, `_gid`) | Tracks page views, sessions, and user behavior | 2 years / 24 hours |
| Mixpanel | Tracks feature usage and user flows | 1 year |
| Error tracking (Sentry) | Logs application errors for debugging | Session |

Analytics data is collected in an **aggregated and anonymized** format wherever possible.

### 2.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences, recent searches, and display settings.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `user_prefs` | Stores UI preferences (e.g., dark mode, notification settings) | 1 year |
| `recent_searches` | Remembers recent service searches for quick access | 30 days |
| `onboarding_complete` | Tracks whether you have completed onboarding steps | 6 months |

### 2.4 Marketing and Advertising Cookies

ServiceHub may use marketing cookies to measure the effectiveness of promotional campaigns and, where you have consented, to deliver relevant advertisements.

| Cookie Name / Tool | Purpose | Duration |

## 5. Your Cookie Controls

### 5.1 Cookie Consent Banner
When you first visit the ServiceHub website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You may:
- **Accept All**: Allow all categories of cookies.
- **Reject Non-Essential**: Allow only strictly necessary cookies.
- **Manage Preferences**: Choose which cookie categories to enable.

### 5.2 Browser Settings
You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:
- View cookies currently stored on your device;
- Block all cookies;
- Block third-party cookies specifically;
- Delete cookies when you close your browser.

**Popular browser guides:**
- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop)
- [Apple Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

Note: Disabling cookies may affect Platform functionality.

### 5.3 Opt-Out Tools
- **Google Analytics Opt-Out**: Install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- **Facebook Ad Preferences**: Manage via [Facebook Ad Settings](https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=ads)

### 5.4 Do Not Track (DNT)
Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. Currently, ServiceHub does not respond to DNT signals from browsers, as there is no industry-standard approach for compliance. We rely instead on our consent management system.
