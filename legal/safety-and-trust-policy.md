# Safety and Trust Policy

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]  
**Last Updated:** [Last Updated Date]

ServiceHub is deeply committed to the safety of every person who uses our platform — whether you are a Customer inviting a Service Provider into your home, or a Provider visiting a new location to perform a job. This Safety and Trust Policy outlines the measures we have in place and the responsibilities of each party in maintaining a safe environment.

---

## 1. Our Commitment to Safety

ServiceHub takes the following steps to promote safety across the Platform:

- **Identity Verification (KYC)**: All users — Customers and Providers — are required to verify their identity before using core platform features (booking or accepting bookings).
- **Provider Screening**: All Service Providers undergo a document review and identity verification process before their profiles are made active.
- **Secure Communications**: All in-app communications are monitored for policy violations and abuse patterns.
- **Fraud Detection**: Our systems monitor for suspicious activity, fraudulent transactions, and policy violations.
- **Community Reporting**: Users can report safety concerns, misconduct, and emergencies directly through the app.

---

## 2. Customer Safety

We take steps to protect Customers booking services through ServiceHub.

### 2.1 Verified Providers Only
Only Providers who have passed our identity verification process may appear in search results or accept bookings. Customers can view the verification status of each Provider on their public profile.

### 2.2 Booking Transparency
Customers receive full booking details, including the Provider's name, photo, verification badge, rating, and service description before confirming a booking.

### 2.3 In-App Communication
All communication between Customers and Providers must happen through the ServiceHub in-app messaging system. We strongly discourage exchanging personal contact information before a booking is confirmed.

### 2.4 Share Your Plans
We recommend that Customers:
- Inform a trusted person of the booking details and expected service duration;
- Keep their phone accessible during the service;
- Trust their instincts — if something feels wrong, it is acceptable to end the engagement and contact ServiceHub.

### 2.5 Safe Service Environment
Customers agree to:
- Ensure the service location is safe and accessible for the Provider;
- Not ask Providers to perform work in unsafe conditions;
- Not harass, threaten, or intimidate Service Providers.

---

## 3. Provider Safety

ServiceHub acknowledges that Providers may be exposed to unfamiliar environments and individuals. We take the following steps to support Provider safety.

### 3.1 Customer Verification
All Customers are required to complete KYC identity verification before they may submit booking requests.

### 3.2 Right to Decline
Providers have the right to decline a booking request if they feel unsafe or believe the request violates platform policies. This will not negatively affect their standing if the declination is reported with a valid reason.

### 3.3 Right to Leave
If a Provider arrives at a service location and feels unsafe or is subjected to harassment, threatening behavior, or unsafe conditions, they have the **right to leave immediately**. Providers should report such incidents through the app.

### 3.4 Recommendations for Providers
- Review Customer profiles and ratings before accepting bookings;
- Inform someone of your schedule and service locations;
- Use the in-app messaging system exclusively;
- Do not carry large amounts of cash to service locations;
- Carry proper identification at all times.

---

## 4. Identity Verification

Identity verification is a cornerstone of safety on ServiceHub.

- **Customers** must complete KYC (government ID + selfie) before booking.
- **Providers** must complete the full Provider Application (government ID, selfie, and relevant documents) before accepting bookings.

Verified users are more accountable for their conduct on the Platform. Verification does not guarantee the character of any user, but significantly reduces the risk of anonymous misuse.

---

## 5. Fraud Prevention

ServiceHub employs the following measures to prevent fraud:

- **Verification checks** on all new accounts to detect fake or duplicate registrations;
- **Secure payment processing** through trusted third-party providers to protect financial data;
- **Booking pattern monitoring** to detect suspicious or abnormal booking activity;
- **Review integrity checks** to identify manipulated or fake reviews;
- **Off-platform solicitation detection** to identify attempts to bypass the platform.

If you suspect fraudulent activity, report it immediately to [Support Email] or through the in-app report function.

---

## 6. Emergency Reporting

### 6.1 Immediate Danger
If you are in **immediate danger**, contact **local emergency services (911)** first. Do not wait for ServiceHub to respond.

### 6.2 In-App Emergency Report
For non-emergency safety incidents that occurred during or in connection with a ServiceHub booking, use the "Report Safety Issue" feature within the booking details screen.

### 6.3 Email Reporting
For sensitive or serious incidents, contact our Trust & Safety team directly at [Support Email] with:
- Your account information;
- A description of the incident;
- Date, time, and booking reference number;
- Any supporting evidence (photos, screenshots, etc.).

### 6.4 Response Time
We aim to respond to safety-related reports within **24 hours** on business days. Urgent safety matters will be escalated and prioritized.

---

## 7. Incident Reporting and Investigation

### 7.1 All Incidents Are Taken Seriously
ServiceHub takes all reported incidents seriously, regardless of severity. We investigate all reports fairly and impartially.

### 7.2 Investigation Process
Upon receiving a safety report:
1. The report is reviewed by our Trust & Safety team;
2. Both parties involved may be contacted for additional information;
3. Available evidence (messages, booking records, photos) is reviewed;
4. Appropriate action is taken based on findings.

### 7.3 Confidentiality
We will not disclose the identity of the person who filed a report to the subject of the investigation unless required by law.

### 7.4 Actions We May Take
Depending on the outcome of the investigation, ServiceHub may:
- Issue a formal warning;
- Temporarily or permanently suspend the account;
- Refer the matter to law enforcement;
- Provide written documentation to support any legal proceedings you may pursue.

---

## 8. Privacy During Safety Investigations

Personal data collected in the course of a safety investigation will be processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

---

## 9. Limitations

While ServiceHub makes every effort to promote safety, we cannot guarantee the conduct or character of any user. ServiceHub is a technology platform and does not supervise, direct, or control the actions of Service Providers or Customers. Users are responsible for their own safety decisions.
