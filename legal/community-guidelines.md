# Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]  
**Last Updated:** [Last Updated Date]

ServiceHub is built on trust, respect, and a shared commitment to quality. These Community Guidelines apply to all users of the Platform — Customers and Service Providers alike — and define the standards of behavior we expect from every member of our community.

Violations of these guidelines may result in warnings, account suspension, or permanent removal from the Platform.

---

## 1. Respectful Behavior

We expect all users to treat each other with dignity and respect.

**You must:**
- Communicate politely and professionally at all times, whether through in-app chat, reviews, or any other channel;
- Be patient and understanding of delays or challenges that may arise during service delivery;
- Address disagreements calmly and constructively;
- Respect the time, effort, and expertise of Service Providers.

**You must not:**
- Use offensive, abusive, threatening, or discriminatory language toward any user;
- Make personal attacks, insults, or belittling remarks;
- Demean users based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, national origin, age, or any other protected characteristic.

---

## 2. Honest Communication

Honesty is the foundation of a trustworthy marketplace.

**You must:**
- Provide accurate information in your profile, service listings, booking requests, and reviews;
- Communicate openly and transparently about service scope, pricing, and expectations;
- Report issues or problems honestly and in good faith.

**You must not:**
- Misrepresent your identity, qualifications, services, or pricing;
- Make false claims about other users in reviews or reports;
- Withhold material information that would affect a booking or service decision.

---

## 3. Safety Expectations

Everyone deserves to feel safe while using ServiceHub.

**All users agree to:**
- Follow all applicable health, safety, and professional standards when performing or receiving services;
- Ensure that service locations are safe, accessible, and appropriate for the work being done;
- Immediately report any safety hazards or concerns to the other party and to ServiceHub.

**Service Providers agree to:**
- Use appropriate safety equipment and follow best practices for their trade;
- Not perform work they are not qualified or equipped to safely complete;
- Respect the privacy, property, and personal space of Customers at all times.

**Customers agree to:**
- Provide a safe working environment for Providers;
- Not request services that may put the Provider at risk or violate safety regulations.

---

## 4. Anti-Harassment Policy

ServiceHub has a **zero-tolerance policy** for harassment of any kind.

**Prohibited harassment includes:**
- Unwanted physical contact or advances;
- Sexual harassment in any form (verbal, written, or physical);
- Persistent unwanted communication after a party has asked to stop;
- Stalking, following, or monitoring another user;
- Threatening behavior, intimidation, or coercion.

Any user who experiences or witnesses harassment should report it immediately through the app or by emailing [Support Email]. ServiceHub will investigate all harassment reports promptly and take appropriate action, including law enforcement referral where warranted.

---

## 5. Anti-Fraud Policy

ServiceHub is committed to maintaining a fraud-free marketplace.

**The following activities are strictly prohibited:**
- Creating fake accounts or impersonating another person;
- Submitting fraudulent booking requests or service listings;
- Manipulating the rating and review system (e.g., self-reviews, paid reviews, coordinated fake reviews);
- Engaging in payment fraud, chargebacks abuse, or unauthorized transactions;
- Circumventing platform fees by arranging off-platform payments;
- Using the Platform to facilitate scams, pyramid schemes, or other deceptive practices.

Users found to be engaging in fraud will have their accounts permanently terminated and may be reported to relevant authorities.

---

## 6. Review Integrity

Reviews and ratings are critical to the trust and quality of ServiceHub. We expect all reviews to be honest and authentic.

**You must:**
- Base reviews solely on your genuine, first-hand experience;
- Write reviews in good faith, even if the experience was negative;
- Report reviews you believe are fake or in violation of these guidelines.

**You must not:**
- Submit reviews for bookings that did not occur;
- Offer or accept payment, discounts, or incentives in exchange for positive reviews;
- Post reviews to retaliate against someone for reasons unrelated to the service;
- Use reviews as a tool for harassment or personal attacks;
- Manipulate another user into changing or removing their honest review.

ServiceHub reserves the right to remove any review that violates these guidelines, and may take action against accounts engaged in review manipulation.

---

## 7. Responsible Use of the Platform

All users agree to use the Platform for its intended purpose only.

**Prohibited uses include:**
- Advertising or promoting businesses, services, or products outside of the ServiceHub service listing framework;
- Contacting other users for purposes unrelated to a legitimate service booking;
- Collecting user data or conducting unauthorized surveys through the Platform;
- Using the Platform as a medium for political campaigns, religious solicitation, or charitable solicitation without ServiceHub's written consent.

---

## 8. Reporting Violations

If you encounter behavior that violates these guidelines:

1. **In-App Report**: Tap the "Report" button on the relevant booking, profile, or message.
2. **Email**: Contact [Support Email] with a description of the incident.
3. **Emergency**: If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (911 in the Philippines) immediately.

All reports are reviewed confidentially. We appreciate your help in keeping the ServiceHub community safe.

---

## 9. Enforcement

ServiceHub may take the following actions in response to violations:

- **Warning**: A formal notice informing the user of the violation.
- **Temporary Suspension**: Access to the Platform is restricted for a defined period.
- **Permanent Removal**: Account is permanently terminated.
- **Legal Action**: Reports to law enforcement or legal proceedings where warranted.

The severity of action taken will depend on the nature and frequency of the violation. ServiceHub's decisions are final, though users may appeal through [Support Email].
