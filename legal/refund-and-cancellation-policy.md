# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** [Effective Date]  
**Last Updated:** [Last Updated Date]

This Refund and Cancellation Policy ("Policy") governs all cancellations, refunds, and related disputes arising from bookings made through the **ServiceHub** platform. This Policy applies to both Customers and Service Providers.

---

## 1. Customer Cancellation Rules

### 1.1 Cancellation More Than 24 Hours Before Scheduled Service
If a Customer cancels a confirmed booking **more than 24 hours** before the scheduled service start time:
- A **full refund** will be issued to the original payment method.
- No cancellation fee will be charged.

### 1.2 Cancellation Between 3 to 24 Hours Before Scheduled Service
If a Customer cancels a confirmed booking **between 3 and 24 hours** before the scheduled service start time:
- A **partial refund** of **70%** of the total booking amount will be issued.
- A cancellation fee of **30%** will be retained to compensate the Provider for reserved time.

### 1.3 Cancellation Less Than 3 Hours Before Scheduled Service
If a Customer cancels a confirmed booking **less than 3 hours** before the scheduled service start time:
- **No refund** will be issued.
- The full amount will be retained to compensate the Provider.

### 1.4 Cancellation After Service Has Commenced
Once a Service Provider has arrived at the service location and work has commenced, cancellations are **not eligible for a refund** unless the Provider is in violation of these Terms or our policies.

---

## 2. Provider Cancellation Rules

### 2.1 Provider Cancellations
Service Providers are expected to honor all confirmed bookings. If a Provider cancels a confirmed booking:
- The Customer will receive a **full refund** of the booking amount.
- The Provider may incur a **cancellation penalty** as outlined in the Provider Terms and Conditions.
- Repeated Provider cancellations may result in account suspension.

### 2.2 Provider No-Shows
If a Service Provider fails to arrive at the service location within **30 minutes** of the scheduled start time without prior notification:
- The booking may be treated as a Provider no-show.
- The Customer will be eligible for a **full refund**.
- The incident will be recorded against the Provider's account.

---

## 3. Customer No-Show Policy

If a Customer is unavailable or unreachable at the service location at the scheduled time:
- The Provider may wait for up to **15 minutes** before marking the booking as a Customer no-show.
- The Customer will **not be eligible for a refund**.
- The Provider will be compensated for the booking and travel time as per the agreed rate.

---

## 4. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be issued under the following circumstances:

| Scenario | Refund Amount |
|---|---|
| Customer cancels > 24 hours before service | 100% |
| Customer cancels 3–24 hours before service | 70% |
| Customer cancels < 3 hours before service | 0% |
| Provider cancels or no-shows | 100% to Customer |
| Service not rendered due to Provider fault | 100% |
| Service quality dispute (resolved in Customer's favor) | Up to 100% |
| Duplicate or erroneous charge | 100% |
| Customer no-show | 0% |

---

## 5. Refund Processing

- Approved refunds are processed within **5 to 10 business days** from the date of approval.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method used at the time of booking.
- ServiceHub is not responsible for delays caused by banks or payment processors.
- Platform fees and third-party payment processing fees may not be refundable in all cases.

---

## 6. Service Quality Complaints

If a Customer is dissatisfied with the quality of service rendered, the following process applies:

### 6.1 Reporting Window
Service quality complaints must be submitted within **48 hours** of the service completion date.

### 6.2 How to File a Complaint
- Open the booking in the ServiceHub app.
- Tap "Report an Issue" or contact support at [Support Email].
- Provide a description of the issue and, where possible, supporting photos or evidence.

### 6.3 Investigation
ServiceHub will review the complaint and may:
- Contact both the Customer and Provider for additional information.
- Review any evidence submitted, chat history, and booking details.

### 6.4 Outcomes
- If the complaint is substantiated, ServiceHub may issue a partial or full refund at its discretion.
- If the complaint is unsubstantiated, no refund will be issued.
- ServiceHub's decision on service quality disputes is **final**.

---

## 7. Dispute Process

### 7.1 Initiating a Dispute
Either party (Customer or Provider) may initiate a dispute within **48 hours** of the booking completion or incident date.

### 7.2 Mediation by ServiceHub
ServiceHub will attempt to mediate disputes fairly and impartially. Both parties will be given an opportunity to present their case.

### 7.3 Resolution Timeframe
ServiceHub aims to resolve disputes within **5 to 7 business days** of receiving all required information.

### 7.4 Off-Platform Transactions
ServiceHub's dispute resolution and refund protections apply **only to bookings and payments processed through the Platform**. Off-platform cash transactions are not covered.
