## 1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide, maintain, and improve our services.

### 1.1 Account Information
When you register for a ServiceHub account, we collect:
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Password (stored in encrypted form)
- Profile photo (optional)
- Date of birth (for age verification where required)
- User role (Customer or Service Provider)

### 1.2 Identity Verification (KYC) Information
For identity verification purposes, we collect:
- Government-issued ID (front and back images)
- Selfie photograph with ID
- Business permit or trade license (for Providers)
- Professional certifications or licenses (for Providers)

This information is used solely for verification and fraud prevention purposes.

### 1.3 Location Data
- **Real-time location**: When you use the app and grant location permissions, we collect your device's GPS coordinates to show nearby providers and facilitate bookings.
- **Service address**: The address you provide for a service booking.

You may disable location access through your device settings, though this may limit certain features.

### 1.4 Booking Information
We collect data related to service bookings, including:
- Service type and description
- Scheduled date and time
- Service address
- Notes and special instructions
- Photos uploaded for the booking
- Booking status and history

### 1.5 Payment Information
We do not store full payment card details on our servers. Payments are processed through third-party payment processors. We may retain:
- Transaction IDs
- Payment status and history
- Partial payment method identifiers (e.g., last 4 digits)
- Refund records

### 1.6 Communications
We collect messages exchanged between Customers and Providers through the in-app chat feature, as well as feedback and support correspondence.

### 1.7 Device and Usage Information
- Device type, operating system, and app version
- IP address
- Browser type (for web access)
- Pages or screens visited
- Time spent on the Platform
- Crash reports and performance logs

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

- **Account Management**: To create and manage your account, verify your identity, and authenticate logins.
- **Service Delivery**: To facilitate bookings between Customers and Service Providers.
- **Provider Verification**: To verify the identity and qualifications of Service Providers.
- **Communications**: To send booking confirmations, notifications, and support messages.
- **Payments**: To process transactions, issue refunds, and maintain financial records.
- **Safety and Trust**: To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and violations of our policies.
- **Platform Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns and improve the Platform's features and performance.
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and court orders in the Philippines and other applicable jurisdictions.
- **Marketing**: With your consent, to send promotional offers, newsletters, and updates about ServiceHub.

## 3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

### 3.1 Between Users
- Customer and Provider profiles (name, photo, ratings) are visible to each other to facilitate bookings.
- Service address and contact information are shared between matched parties for the purpose of completing a booking.

### 3.2 Service Providers and Partners
We share information with third-party vendors who help us operate the Platform, including:
- Cloud hosting and storage providers
- Payment processors
- Analytics platforms
- Customer support tools

These partners are contractually bound to protect your data and use it only as directed by us.

### 3.3 Legal Authorities
We may disclose your information when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of ServiceHub, its users, or the public.

### 3.4 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified of any such change and the applicable privacy policy.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:
- **Account data**: Retained for the duration of your account and up to 5 years after account deletion for legal compliance purposes.
- **KYC documents**: Retained for up to 5 years as required by anti-fraud and legal compliance obligations.
- **Booking records**: Retained for up to 3 years.
- **Chat messages**: Retained for up to 1 year from the date of the conversation.

You may request earlier deletion of your data, subject to our legal obligations.
